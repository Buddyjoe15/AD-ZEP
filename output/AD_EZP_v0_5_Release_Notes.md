# AD-EZP v0.5

- Fabrication is narrower and follows the ship on screen. On small displays it attaches above or below the ship and scrolls.
- The window appears immediately on selecting the ship. A brief input guard prevents the selection tap from triggering a build button where the window overlaps the ship.
- New Earths have no scattered Element P discoveries, chests, loose items, or generated buildings.
- A testing zone left of the ship holds one Field Cap, Simple Backpack, Chest, Wall, Field Generator, Repair Station and Sensor Station.
- Archive signals, scrap deposits, fabrication, Utility Spider construction, expedition logging and transit remain playable.

Verification: headless Chromium desktop and touch emulation, full expedition transit, save/load and guard checks. Test results and screenshots are in the source archive. Existing imported saves preserve their recorded world objects; the new placement applies to newly generated Earths. Physical mobile devices, Safari and Firefox were not tested.
