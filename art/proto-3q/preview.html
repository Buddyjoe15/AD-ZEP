Utility Spider: three-quarter prototype

Rendered by tools/spider-3q.mjs from a 3D model built in code: 8 facings, each its own render, at 2 art px per world px (today's art is 1). Everything is compared on the game's Woodlands grass. Shadows darken the ground by 55%, as the engine does.

At game zoom: today (left) and the prototype (right)

1.5 screen px per world px is zoom 1 on a high-density screen (the renderer caps density at 1.5). 3.3 is the closest zoom the game allows (2.2 × 1.5).


Walking in a ring: today (left) and the prototype (right), 2.2 screen px per world px

Eight Spiders of each team walk a circle, so every facing shows. In the prototype, Spiders nearer the bottom of the screen are drawn over those behind them.
